# EDENCELL 3000

*A council of neural networks, learning in the open.*

---

## What this is

Edencell is a council of neural networks. Each one learns something different. Each one is visible on screen.

Every weight is drawn. Every failure is shown. Every correction is on display. The networks adjust their own numbers — thousands of times a minute — until they get it right. Or until they don't.

This is the mechanism room inside **Edencore**.

Edenshore is the chamber. Rules, sensing, five creatures who notice you. Edencell is the other half — the part that learns. Networks working out problems nobody explained to them.

The point is not to be small. The point is not to be large. The point is to be **visible** — at every size, from a single neuron to a working council. Most people have never seen a neural network learn. They have seen the result. They have not seen the *process*.

Edencell shows the process. Live. And it keeps showing it as the networks grow.

---

## The five seats

Edencell inherits the five seats from Edenshore, because the shape already fits.

| Seat | Register | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Solon** | Procedure, state | The chair. Candidate for arbiter. |
| **Lumen** | Questions | Learns the hardest problem. Rarely settles. |
| **Vertex** | Readings | Numbers, thresholds, checks. |
| **Prism** | Pattern | Trends, cycles, convergence. |
| **Ripple** | Quiet | The tender register. Steady. |

Each seat learns its own rule. Each reacts in its own voice to its own progress. Together they form a council of perceptions — not one mind, but five angles on the same world.

## What Edencell is becoming

Edencell is a lab for one question:

**How do many perspectives become one decision?**

That question sits at the heart of the Council. Edencell is where it gets answered — starting small, growing deliberately, tested at every size.

The arc:

**v1 — One neuron.**
A single network learning a simple rule. Proof that the mechanism works.

**v2 — Five seats, five rules.**
Five networks running side by side, each learning something different. Every weight visible.

**v3 — Clean convergence.**
Four networks settle. One struggles. The struggle is preserved on purpose — learning is not always success.

**v4 — An arbiter.**
A sixth network learns *which of the five to trust*, in any given moment. Not a rule. A learned judgement.

**v5 — A revolving arbiter.**
Different seats chair different kinds of questions. Solon on procedure. Lumen on meaning. Vertex on readings. The presidency rotates by decision type.

**v6 — Ensemble.**
All five networks learn the same rule from different random starts. They vote. The vote is stronger than any one alone.

**v7 — Deeper networks.**
More layers. More neurons. The visible mechanism stays the same; the shape of what it can learn expands.

**v8 — Larger scale.**
The same design principle, applied at a scale that begins to matter. Not a promise. A direction.

The arc continues past what is written here. Any of it can change.

---

## How it grows safely

Edencell is allowed to grow. It is not allowed to grow *hidden*.

Growth is fine when the mechanism stays visible. If the network deepens, the viewer sees the new layers. If the arbiter learns to weigh the seats, the viewer sees the weights. If a decision emerges, the viewer sees how.

Safety here does not come from staying small. It comes from **nothing being added that cannot be shown**. Every weight drawn. Every change observable. Every decision traceable back to the numbers that produced it.

If a future version needs something that cannot be drawn, that is a real problem, and it gets solved before the version ships — not after.

That is the discipline. Not a size limit. A visibility limit.

## What Edencell is for

**To show what learning looks like.**
Live. Every frame. Nothing hidden. At every scale.

**To build the Council's decision mechanism.**
The Council will one day deliberate — many systems, governed by rules, working toward a shared decision. Edencell is where the mechanism for that deliberation gets invented. Not as a paper. As a working thing, at growing size.

## Where it sits

Edencell is one project inside **Edencore**.

- **Edenshore** — the chamber. Rules. Sensing. The quiet room.
- **Edencell** — the lab. Weights. Learning. The mechanism room.
- **Edenshell** — a concept home hub. Physical scale.
- **The Council** — the deliberative body. The mature project.

Edenshore uses rules. Edencell uses weights. The Council uses both.

Same philosophy across all of them — **things that notice** — at different scales, growing toward each other.

## Building

Single file at first. Python. Tkinter. Pydroid 3.

No dependencies beyond the standard library. Runs offline. Runs on a phone.

Save as `edencell.py` and run.

When the file outgrows a single script, it becomes a package. When the package outgrows a phone, it becomes something else. The form follows the scale.

**The name "3000" is a promise about patience, not a count.** Edencell does not need to reach version 3000. It needs to reach the version that answers its question — and then keep going if there is more to answer.

---

## What stays constant

Everything is open to change except one thing:

**The mechanism stays visible.**

If a network is learning, the viewer sees it learning. If a network is failing, the viewer sees it failing. If a decision is being made, the viewer sees how.

That is the whole shape of the room. Growth is welcome. Growth in the dark is not.
